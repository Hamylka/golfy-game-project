# Props, ball collisions, ambient life (M4 plan, PRs 4.2-4.4, 4.5)

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md (develop @ bc81fcf) so it is read only when props, placement, collisions or ambient life are touched. The plan itself is `Docs/Plans/m4-plan.md`.

- **Props (`Generation`, decided in the M4 plan, `Docs/Plans/m4-plan.md`):** procedural meshes in C# from a shared kit (`PropMeshKit`), palette vertex colours, flat facets, no modelled prefabs: olive trees, cypresses, round bushes, rocks (round white rocks by water), dry-stone walls, a tile-roofed shed, a bench, a small decorative stone bridge over a pond, one or two traces of human presence, and at most one landmark (chapel or lighthouse) at the island's edge. Static props share one mesh on the land material; animated foliage stays in it through a sway weight, or falls back to one foliage mesh of its own (at most +2 draw calls in all). `PropPlacement` runs after the hazards on its own random stream (salt 7), so props never move an island, a slope or a hazard, with whole-centimetre tests, and outputs `CourseLayout.Props`. Clusters, low density (par 3 2-3 clusters, par 4 3-5, par 5 4-7, tunable in `LevelGenSettings`, reviewed on the phone so the island doesn't feel empty), empty ground between them. Never in a shot corridor (2 m either side of every direct, cut and safe line), a landing capsule, the tee, the green and 1.5 m around it, a hazard or the +X end kept for M5, and never over the fairway: a prop's whole collision footprint plus `PropRules.fairwayMargin` (0.3 m) stays off fairway ground (director, PR 4.4). The bridge's own footprint stays clear of every corridor and capsule, so the pond's risk and the safe route never change. Built in PR 4.3: a prop is tested by the true reach of its collision shapes (each candidate is built from its mesh seed), and a tall prop's shadow (direction and length from `Main`'s sun, `PropRules.shadowHeading`/`shadowLength`) stays out of the corridors too; a cluster starts where 1 m of free ground surrounds it, at an even integer draw over the valid starts (director: Q2 A), one sub-stream per cluster; themes olive grove, cypress line along a wall, bushes and rocks, edge rocks or wall (never by a pond), round white rocks on a pond's rim. The variant is picked on the bare course; the checks then play with the props and the pole, and drop every prop a check shot struck when a check fails with them (director: Q1 A), never the course. The terrain view builds the same props from the same seeds, so the drawn props are the shapes the ball strikes. Built in PR 4.4: shed, bench, bridge, traces and landmarks come from the kit and are placed on sub-streams keyed above the clusters', so the 4.3 clusters stay bit-identical: built clusters (a shed or a bench facing the course, with its trace: a bicycle on the shed's wall, laundry between two free posts behind it, a bucket by a bench) are extra to the density's clusters; a rake lies in a bunker (flat, it never collides); the bridge (one box collider, water to parapet top) spans a pond wherever its own span and banks, with every keep-out and its shadow, stay clear of the corridors and capsules (director: option A; it appears on about 6% of par 3s, 1.5% of par 4s and 2.5% of par 5s); the landmark stands on a promontory of the island's edge away from the play axis. Frequencies in `LevelGenSettings.props`. Strategic obstacles on the direct and cut lines: see *Strategic obstacles* below.
- **Ball collisions (`Gameplay`, decided in the M4 plan):** inside `BallSimulation`, deterministic, against simple shapes held by each prop (cylinders, spheres, boxes), found through a 2 m grid. In flight the first hit is found up front, like the landing; trunks, rocks, walls and built props are hard and bounce the ball, crowns and bushes are soft: a raised crown lets the ball on into its leaves, slowed, to fall out under the tree, a bush bounces it off weakly (built in PR 4.2, `BallObstacles`; a hit cap of 8 ends any loop by dropping the ball). The bridge is one block: the ball never rolls on its deck. Flat traces on the ground (a rake) don't collide. The flag pole counts in flight only: a slow ball dropping off it falls by the hole and `HoleRim` decides, a fast one bounces off, so no pole hit guarantees a chip-in; a rolling ball ignores the pole, and putting is unchanged. The course checks and the ace sweep run with props and collisions on; a prop that breaks a check is dropped, not the course rerolled.
- **Ambient life (`Rendering`, decided in the M4 plan):** visual only, never read by the simulation. In the shader: the flag flutters calmly (the pole never moves), foliage rustles, water highlights fade in and out. On the CPU: a few birds take off as the ball passes. Butterflies and a drifting cloud shadow are tried on the phone and kept only if approved. No compute shaders or threads (WebGL).
- **Fork spine props (F3.4, `feature/fairway-spine-props`, split fairway plan §4, hub: Q1-Q4 A):** on a fork course, one small cluster on the spine, extra to the density's clusters, from its own sub-stream of the props stream keyed after every other (`PropPlacement.SpineBase` = 160) and placed last, so every other prop is bit-identical with and without it. Theme 50 / 50 (`props.spineWeights`, a setting the director may retune): a short cypress line along the spine, or bushes and rocks led by an olive only where one fits its reach and shadow (Q2 A); a cypress or an olive with no room becomes a bush (Q3 A). Every keep-out applies unchanged: members are accepted by `PropZones.Fits` (corridors 2 m either side of every line and their shadows, capsules, `fairwayMargin` 0.3 m off both branches). The free strip is searched on rough and clear of play only (between the branches the corridors reach further than the fairway margin), the full test then decides. Under Main's sun a cypress's shadow reaches a corridor on almost every spine, so spines are mostly bushes and rocks (measured: a cypress fits on 8 of 92 forks); changing that means a narrower shadow rule on spines, not decided. Fixed with it (Q1 A): the props' landing capsules follow the fork's rest offset, as the safe route's do.
- **Strategic obstacles (decided in `Docs/Plans/strategic-obstacles-plan.md`, `docs/strategic-obstacles-plan`, director 2026-11-12: Q1 B, Q2 B, Q3 B, Q4 A, Q5 "plutôt B mais pas dans 100% des cas", Q6 A, Q7 B, Q8 A; to build in S1 `feature/strategic-zones` and S2 `feature/strategic-props`):** one strategic group per course where there is room, drawn at `props.strategicChance` 60%, 1-3 props, on its own sub-stream of the props stream (`PropPlacement.StrategicBase` = 170), placed last, so every other prop stays bit-identical (spines aside, see below). Zones: fork spines, split bands, rough islands (small props only: a rock or a low bush, reach at most 0.5 m, `fairwayMargin` 0.15 m on ovals; no shape change) and the `Cut` line at a dogleg's inside corner (beside the `CornerCarry` hazard, never on it). Kinds: trees, bushes and rocks; sheds and walls only on spines and corners whose ground holds their footprint and shadow. Par 3: through rough islands only. Strategic props may stand in the **direct and cut corridors**. Every landing capsule, the tee, the green and 1.5 m around it, hazards, the +X end, the fairway with its margin and each safe leg's take-off and landing stay strict. Under a safe leg's middle a strategic prop is allowed only where the leg's planned arc passes `props.strategicSafeClearance` (0.3 m) over its top plus the ball's radius, along its whole footprint. The arc height comes from a shot model mirrored in `LevelGenSettings` and tested against `BallSimulation`. A new check, `SafeLegsFly`, plays each safe leg's first flight at its planned power with props on and requires no strike (bounces and rolls not counted). A strike drops the prop, never the course, so the reroll counts can't move. Blocking (Q5): by default the group leaves the line playable (1.5 m clear across the corridor, or lower than the full-power apex). A full block is allowed on `props.strategicFullBlockChance` 25% of placements, never on a par 3 or on a straight course's tee shot, and only on a cut line or a fork's risky-branch direct line where the safe route reaches the green in at most par − 1 shots. Strategic props are props with a flag on `CourseProp`, not hazards: no `HazardRole`, not counted in the hazard counts. Spines (Q7 B): a cypress's shadow may cross the direct and cut corridors (the tree stays out of every corridor, its shadow off the safe legs and capsules). Measured: a cypress then fits on about 55% of forks, against about 9%, so spine props move on fork courses. Aim preview unchanged: `PredictHit` already stops the dots at the first contact, crowns included, with the same mark for soft and hard props.
- **Strategic zones and `SafeLegsFly` (S1, `feature/strategic-zones`, hub 2026-11-12):** built with no strategic prop placed, so every checked course is bit-identical (variant, checks, drops and every prop, against `CheckedCourseReferenceBefore`, captured on develop b3e4a8b; S2 reuses it). `PropZones.ClearOfPlay` and `Fits` keep today's strict rule for every prop; `StrategicClearOfPlay` and `FitsStrategic` are the strategic rule S2 calls: the direct and cut corridors are open to the prop and its shadow, and a safe leg's corridor only where `UnderSafeArc` holds. That is: the footprint's whole along-span lies strictly between the leg's take-off and landing, and over it the planned arc keeps the ball's **bottom** `props.strategicSafeClearance` (0.3 m) above the prop's top (the centre passes clearance plus radius over it; hub: (b)). The arc is a downward parabola, so testing the span's two ends is exact. The planned arc (`PlannedArc`, Generation) mirrors `BallisticArc.ForCarry`: with a fixed launch angle θ (`ArcFor` scales the carry by the lie, never the angle) and flat carry C, the ball's bottom stands h0 + x·tanθ·(1 − x/C), gravity cancelling out; the carry that lands at the leg's end comes in closed form, C = L / (1 − (h1 − h0)/(L·tanθ)), and the power from inverting `CarryAt`, clamped to 0-1. Safe legs start on fairway (carry scale 1, held by a test), so no lie is mirrored. `LevelGenSettings` mirrors `launchAngle` (36, as `ShotSettings.asset`; the code default 40 was wrong for this project, caught by `LevelGenShotModelTests`) and `ballRadius` (0.12). The leg's ground heights come from `CourseHeightField`, the ball's own ground, built only on the first strategic test. `CourseChecks.SafeLegsFly` (flag `SafeLegHit`, run only with props on) plays each safe leg's first flight at its planned power through `PredictHit`, the search `Shoot` runs, so nothing is stepped; a prop strike fails it and names the prop, so `KeepProps` drops it. **The flag pole doesn't count** (hub: (a)): only a prop can be dropped, the pole is the approach check's business, and a pole-only failure would fail a course no drop can fix. On today's checked courses (seeds 0-49 per par) no safe leg's first flight strikes a prop, so the check drops nothing. Settings added for S2 (read by nothing yet but the clearance): `strategicChance` 60, `strategicMembers` 1-3, `strategicFullBlockChance` 25, `strategicOvalMargin` 0.15.
